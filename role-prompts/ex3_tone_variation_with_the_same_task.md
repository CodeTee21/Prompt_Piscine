Objective: Control tone and style with explicit instructions.

    Choose one task (e.g., "Write an email inviting a candidate to an interview").

    Write two prompts:

        One for a friendly tone

        One for a professional tone
        
# Prompt 1: friendly tone

Hi Ayo!

Great news—our team took a look at your portfolio, and we really loved your style! We’re super excited about the Graphic Designer role and would love to hop on a call (or meet in person) to get to know you better.

Do you have some time later this week to hang out and chat about the position? Let us know what works for you!

Can’t wait to meet you!

Best,

The Creative Team.

# Prompt 2: Professional Tone

Dear Ayo,

Thank you for your interest in the Graphic Designer position. After reviewing your application, we would like to invite you to interview with our hiring committee to discuss your qualifications in more detail.

Please provide your availability for a 30-minute interview during the upcoming business week. Once your response is received, we will send a formal calendar invitation with the meeting details.

We look forward to hearing from you.

Sincerely,

Human Resources Department.


# Compare the outputs and note how the tone changes while the content stays consistent.
1. Greeting: The friendly prompt uses Hi ayo while professional prompt uses Dear Ayo as  formal greetings
2. The Vibe: The friendly prompt tone is super excited and professional prompt focuses on the candidate qualifications
